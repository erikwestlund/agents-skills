# Writing

Conventions that apply to everything written for Erik, in any project. A
project's own style guide can add rules; it does not replace these.

## Oxford comma

Always put a comma before the final "and" or "or" in a list of three or more.

- Yes: "The study, its team, and its findings are made up for this demo."
- No: "The study, its team and its findings are made up for this demo."
- Two items take no comma: "The course and its materials."

This covers UI labels and copy, docs, plans, briefs, messages between agents,
and commit messages.

When you fix existing text, change only the commas unless you were asked for
more. Don't sweep generated or seeded content for commas unless the task asks
for it.
